Project brief

My graduate team is working with Horizon Farm Credit’s Agricultural Lending business area to investigate how internal data and reporting support employees’ work.

The project is at an early stage. This page describes the scope and questions I’m bringing into the work; findings and recommendations will be added as the project develops.

Context
Graduate team client project
Users
Employees in Agricultural Lending
Focus
Internal data, reports, dashboards, and work processes
01 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Research Focus

I want to understand the decisions employees make and the steps around them, including where information comes from, how reports are used, and what happens when something is unclear.

WORKFLOWS

Workflows

What does someone need to accomplish? Which tools and reports do they use along the way?

INFORMATION

Information Needs

Which information do employees rely on, and how do they decide whether it is current or trustworthy?

FRICTION

Barriers and Workarounds

Where do people switch tools, repeat steps, or ask someone else for help?

02 / WHAT COMES NEXT

Next Steps

The next steps are to clarify the scope with the client, plan research with my team, and learn from employees’ actual workflows.

I’ll document the research approach, my individual contributions, findings, and any design implications once that work is completed and appropriate to share.
